### **Best American Food Rides to Ride**

To spend some time on a food trip, the following are by no means all of the rider-friendly routes:

### **The Southern Comfort Trail**

Start in Georgia or Alabama, and ride up and down small towns that are known for barbecue, fried chicken, and soul food. You will have roadside dineries, market kiosks, and local restaurants, where centuries of recipes have been kept.

### **The Coastal Seafood Circuit**

Go to the Gulf Coast or the East Coast of Maine, or Massachusetts. You can take your electric bike motorcycle to the office at the coast, and take a rest at the clam shacks, fresh lobster rolls, or Cajun seafood boils.

### **Farm-to-Table Backroads**

Explore the wine country and farmlands, and countryside valleys with an over-the-road farmers' market and farm stands. Eat local food, local cheese, and homemade baked goods in their home location.

These tracks are showing not only dishes, but also natural landscapes, crowds of people, and culture, that local dishes are unique.
