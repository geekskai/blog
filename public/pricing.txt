# Geekskai Audio Toolkit Pricing

Currency: USD
Billing model: Flat subscription; no per-seat pricing
Processing model: Audio processing runs locally in the user's browser
Payment processor: PayPal Business
Current purchase status: Basic and Pro recurring subscriptions are available through secure PayPal checkout
Refund policy: One 14-day full-refund window applies to an account's first successful subscription payment; renewals and later subscriptions do not start a new window

## Free

- Price: $0
- Batch limit: 1 file
- Formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, and M4A
- Includes: Two-pass LUFS normalization

## Basic

- Monthly: $10 per month
- Annual: $96 per year, equivalent to $8 per month
- Annual discount: 20% compared with 12 monthly payments
- Batch limit: Up to 20 files
- Includes: Everything in Free, ZIP export
- Support: Email response within 2 business days

## Pro

- Monthly: $25 per month
- Annual: $240 per year, equivalent to $20 per month
- Annual discount: 20% compared with 12 monthly payments
- Batch limit: Up to 50 files
- Includes: Everything in Basic, ZIP export
- Support: Priority response within 1 business day
