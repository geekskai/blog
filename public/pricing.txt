# Geekskai Audio Credits Pricing

Currency: USD
Credit rate: 1 Credit per minute of combined input audio, rounded up once per batch
Processing model: Audio processing runs locally in the user's browser
Payment processor: PayPal Business
Current purchase status: PAYG and Regular monthly purchases use secure PayPal checkout
Refund policy: Full refund within 14 days only when no Credits from that payment were consumed

## Free

- Price: $0
- Credits: 30 per UTC day
- Expiry: Next 00:00 UTC; no rollover
- Batch limit: 1 file

## Pay As You Go

- Price: $14 one-time
- Credits: 480
- Expiry: 365 days after payment
- Batch limit: Up to 50 files
- Includes: ZIP export

## Regular

- Monthly: $29 per month
- Credits: 2,800 after each successful monthly payment
- Expiry: End of the current billing period; no rollover
- Batch limit: Up to 50 files
- Includes: ZIP export

## Credit use

- Daily Free Credits are consumed first, then subscription Credits, then Pay As You Go Credits.
- Failed or cancelled processing consumes no Credits.
- Partially successful batches consume only the rounded combined duration of successful files.
- Public third-party downloader allowances remain separate and do not change after a Credit purchase.
