# Use PayPal approval with site-owned subscription management

Geekskai will embed the PayPal subscription experience but grant paid access only after server verification of the subscription, allowed Plan, payment state, and account binding. Geekskai retains its own account billing page and cancellation action; cancellation stops future renewal while Paid-Through Access lasts until the current paid period ends, and PayPal remains available for payment-method management and external cancellation.
