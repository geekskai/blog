# Use PayPal with Geekskai as merchant

Geekskai will migrate recurring payment processing from the Creem Merchant of Record model to PayPal while remaining the direct Merchant. This accepts responsibility for tax treatment, customer disclosures, receipts or invoices, refunds, disputes, and payment support in exchange for using the approved PayPal Business account; production checkout remains disabled until those responsibilities and PayPal live eligibility are verified.
